JBC Speech (online version) - IPA library for Microsoft Word
==========================================================

This small installer adds the JBC Speech tab to Word. The library itself loads from
the JBC Speech website each time you open it, so it needs an internet connection,
and it updates itself automatically. Nothing runs in the background on your PC.

INSTALL
1. Before unzipping: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK.
   (This stops Windows warning that the files came from the internet.)
2. Unzip it (right-click > Extract All). Don't run it from inside the zip.
3. Close Word, then double-click "Install JBC Speech (online).cmd".
   If Windows still says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Open Word. Choose the JBC Speech tab, then IPA Library.

USING IT
- Click in your document, then click a symbol to insert it.
- Shift-click a symbol (marked with a music note) to hear it.
- The List | Chart switch at the top changes the layout. Widen the pane to see the
  whole consonant chart at once.
- For the best-looking symbols, use an IPA font such as Doulos SIL or Charis SIL.

UNINSTALL (any one of these; nothing to type)
- Open the Settings app, click Apps, then Installed apps. Find "JBC Speech (online)",
  click the "..." button next to it, then Uninstall.
  (Windows 10: Settings > Apps > Apps & features > click it > Uninstall.)
- Or double-click "Uninstall JBC Speech (online).cmd" in this folder.

On a work computer, check with your IT department first. They can also add the
library for you without this installer.
