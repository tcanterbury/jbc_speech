Thank you for helping make the IPA Library better. It's a work in progress, and ideas and bug reports from the people who use it, especially SLPs, are what shape it.

Please don't include client or patient information. Feedback is posted publicly, so others can see it and add their thoughts. Describe the symbol or step instead of pasting real transcriptions.

Ideas and bug reports are collected on GitHub. Each button opens a short form with a few prompts to fill in.
