Audio credits

Recordings by Prof. John Esling and Jill House, sourced from the International Phonetic Association’s interactive IPA chart. Copyright remains with the respective rights holders. Retrieved November 26, 2026.

The original MP3 recordings are included unchanged for personal, noncommercial use. No trimming, conversion, or audio processing has been applied.

The interactive chart’s credits and licensing page provides Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–NoDerivatives 4.0 terms: credit the creators, retain the license information, and do not redistribute adapted recordings or use them commercially under this license.

John Esling’s source resources: University of Victoria IPA charts. Jill House’s recordings are credited by the official chart to Sounds of the IPA and its vowel chart resources.

Not every symbol has a recording from each speaker. Some clips contain example syllables, words, or contrasting sounds. Where the chart supplies multiple examples, they play in sequence.

This add-in is independent and is not endorsed by the speakers or the International Phonetic Association. Per-file source URLs, sizes, and SHA-256 checksums are preserved in src/assets/audio/provenance.json in the project folder.
