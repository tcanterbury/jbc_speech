JBC Speech

In Word, choose JBC Speech → IPA Library to show or hide the library.

Place your cursor in the document, then click a symbol. Selected text is replaced. The library stays open while you type or insert symbols.

All categories appear in one scrollable view. Search by symbol, phonetic name, category, or Unicode code. Use “Jump to” to reach a category.

Use the List | Chart switch to change the layout. Chart lays the symbols out like the IPA chart: consonants by place and manner (voiceless left, voiced right, shaded cells impossible) and vowels on the trapezoid. In a narrow pane the consonant table is split into four parts; widen the pane to see it whole. Your choice is remembered.

Combining diacritics attach to the preceding letter. Place the cursor immediately after that letter. For a tie bar, insert it between two letters. Dotted circles are never inserted.

The library covers standard IPA categories, with convenience affricates and transcription brackets. It does not include the separate extIPA inventory. Choose J. Esling or J. House in the Voice selector, then Shift-click a symbol marked ♪ to hear it without inserting. Shift+Enter and Shift+Space also work. Use Stop audio to stop. Some recordings include example syllables or words. The recordings are by Prof. John Esling and Jill House, with our heartfelt thanks.
